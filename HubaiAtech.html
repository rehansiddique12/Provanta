Hubai Atech Biotechnology

Hubai Atech Biotechnology Co., Ltd.

Hubai Atech Biotechnology Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise that focuses on the research and large-scale production of natural yeast and its enzymatic hydrolysis products.


Founded in 2009, the company's mission is "to focus on safe and healthy food and accompany a beautiful and long life," supported by bio-fermentation technology and enzyme engineering technology.


The company produces more than 50,000 tons of yeast and its biological products annually, covering high-activity dry yeast, yeast hydrolysate, yeast extract, yeast cell wall, yeast powder, yeast selenium, microecological liquid animal health preparations, and functional protein peptides.


The company is accelerating the construction of a global sales network and service system, committed to providing professional and high-quality services to domestic and foreign customers in multiple fields such as animal nutrition, bio-fermentation, and organic farming.

Provanta Pharma is a leading Animal Health company in Pakistan, representing multiple trusted brands. We offer a comprehensive premium animal health and nutrition products designed to enhance the productivity and wellbeing of poultry and livestock. Our commitment to quality and and innovation has made us a trusted partner in the veterinary and livestock sectors.
