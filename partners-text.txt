Aminorich Nutrients B.V.
Aminorich Nutrients B.V. is a Netherlands-based animal-health and nutrition company engaged in developing, manufacturing, and marketing research-based veterinary products, nutritional supplements, and feed additives. The company serves the poultry, dairy cattle, swine, aquaculture, equine, camel, and companion-animal sectors.
Its product portfolio includes phytogenic feed additives, vitamins and minerals, amino-acid supplements, probiotics, prebiotics, biosecurity products, herbal formulations, and selected veterinary pharmaceuticals. These products are designed to support animal health, immunity, growth, feed efficiency, reproduction, milk production, egg production, and overall farm performance.
Aminorich Nutrients B.V. places strong emphasis on research, quality control, product safety, and internationally recognized production practices. The company states that its manufacturing and quality-management systems operate according to GMP and HACCP standards, supported by laboratory testing and quality checks throughout the production process. It supplies its products through international business and distribution partners in different markets.
